Native Cultures Before European Arrival

Use these regional landing pages to investigate Native societies through the GRAPES framework: Geography, Religion, Achievement, Politics, Economics, and Social Structure.

Important: A region is not one culture. Make specific claims about the people or society your source describes. To make a regional claim, compare evidence from at least two distinct communities.

GRAPES lenses

Geography

The relationship between people and the natural world.

Religion

The shared ideas, values, and practices that guide how people think about life, the world, and their place in it.

Achievement

The inventions and ideas that people develop to solve problems and improve their lives.

Politics

How people make decisions as a group.

Economics

The study of how we make, trade, and use goods and services.

Social Structure

How people are divided into groups and roles.
