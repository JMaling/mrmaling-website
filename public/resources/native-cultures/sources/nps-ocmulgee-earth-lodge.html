The Ocmulgee Earth Lodge

When visitors go into the Earth Lodge, they get a view of the ancient past. The Earth Lodge is a reconstructed council chamber of the Mississippian culture. Its walls and ceiling were reconstructed in the late 1930s. Crews from the Civilian Conservation Corps and the Works Project Administration did the work. Archeologist A.R. Kelly directed them. The clay floor inside is the original floor built by Native people. Carbon dating puts it at the year 1015.
