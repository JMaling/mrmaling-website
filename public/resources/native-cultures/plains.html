Early U.S. History Native Cultures Presentation

Plains

These sources focus on Mandan, Hidatsa, and Arikara village societies along the Missouri and Knife Rivers. They show that Plains life before European arrival included farming towns, earthlodges, long-distance trade, and complex social organizations.

Use the Khan Academy reading for the broad region, then compare it with the more specific Knife River sources.

Watch for a stereotype: Plains societies were not all horse-mounted nomadic bison hunters. Before horses spread across the Plains, many communities lived in permanent farming villages.
