Early U.S. History Native Cultures Presentation

Northeast

These sources combine Haudenosaunee voices with archaeological evidence from earlier Hopewell communities. Together they show the region’s long history of farming, trade, political organization, earthworks, and kinship.

Use the Khan Academy reading to identify major regional patterns, then use these sources for specific evidence.

Research reminder: Hopewell communities and the Haudenosaunee lived in different periods and were not the same people. Label your evidence carefully.
