Early U.S. History Native Cultures Presentation

Southeast

These sources focus on Mississippian and earlier mound-building societies, especially Ocmulgee and Poverty Point. Archaeological evidence reveals farming, trade, earthworks, political hierarchy, and ceremonial life.
